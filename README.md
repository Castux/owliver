# Owliver, space tower janitor

Equipped with his JanitPack 500 and his überRepair plutonium powered repair gun, Owliver's job is to fix whatever goes wrong in the tower. Seven hand-drawn levels, and unbearable repetitive music.

Made in 48 hours for **Ludum Dare #12** (August 2008); this is the slightly fixed post-competition version.

It was made with [LÖVE](https://love2d.org/) 0.5 and won't run as is on current versions. It was imported in 2026 from the archive published on my old site; there was no version control history.

The original builds (2008–2010) are in the [releases](../../releases).
